A good first connection

1. Open a room. Share its invitation code or link privately. Invitation holders can join immediately. For Nearby or a separate private group, use Other connection options.

2. Choose who stays. Hosts can remove a participant, lock a room or create a new invitation. Removing and closing the invitation prevents re-entry with that old code. A new anonymous identity cannot be permanently identified as the same person.

3. Choose your permissions. Your microphone and screen start off. Share your screen only when you choose. Remote control still needs the screen owner's separate approval.

Device audio. Share sound from this device separately from your microphone. Some apps block audio capture.

A closer look. Zoom with the + and − buttons, your mouse wheel while viewing, Ctrl / Cmd + wheel while controlling, or a two-finger pinch. Pan view moves the picture locally; Fit shows the whole screen again. Zoom does not improve the incoming resolution. Approved controllers can enable Touch drag for one-finger dragging; a second finger releases the remote button and returns to pinch zoom.

4. Read the real measurements. Connection details show actual resolution, frame rate and network data. Picture quality and reachability depend on your devices, network and relay availability.

Android companion: phone screen sharing uses the system capture permission. Phone control requires Glance-Port Accessibility, a full-screen share and separate owner approval. Stop from the sharing notification at any time. Phone capture targets 12 fps up to a 1920-pixel long edge; protected content remains protected.

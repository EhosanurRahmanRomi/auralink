A good first connection

1. Start on the same network. Create a room on Windows or Mac and share its invitation with your other devices.

2. Verify the host. The room uses a local certificate. Compare its fingerprint through a trusted channel before accepting it.

3. Choose your permissions. Join after approval, then enable audio, camera or screen sharing. Desktop control requires another approval.

4. Read the real measurements. Connection details show actual WebRTC resolution, frame rate and network data. Internet access needs a reachable host; no relay is included.

Android companion: phone screen sharing uses the system capture permission. Phone control requires Auralink Accessibility, a full-screen share and separate owner approval. Stop from the sharing notification at any time. Phone capture targets 12 fps up to 1080p; protected content remains protected.
